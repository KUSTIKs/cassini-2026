# 12th CASSINI Hackathon: EU Space for Peace & Resilience

Organiser: EUSPA (EU Agency for the Space Programme), run by "CASSINI Hackathons & Mentoring".
Platform: TAIKAI, https://taikai.network/en/cassinihackathons/hackathons/space-for-peace-resilience/overview
Official site: https://www.cassini.eu/hackathons
Contact: hello@hackathons.cassini.eu (event questions), prizes@euspa.europa.eu (formal / rules questions)

## Format
- Runs at the same time in **10 locations**: Bulgaria, Czech Republic, Finland, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Lithuania, Poland, Romania, Ukraine.
- **Stage 1, local hackathon (weekend):** each location picks **one winner**, which goes to Stage 2. Locations may also hand out their own local prizes, which aren't EUSPA prizes.
- **Stage 2, Demo Day (online, English):** the 10 local winners pitch to an expert jury, which picks the top 3.
- Language is English. Some talks may be in the local language.

## Prizes
- **Overall (EUSPA):** 1st €5,000 · 2nd €3,000 · 3rd €1,000. All three also get **CASSINI Mentoring**: up to 100 hours over at most 6 months, to turn the project into a business.
- **Local:** each location has its own prizes (see the location pages on cassini.eu).

## Teams and eligibility
- Teams of **3 to 8 members**. Everyone registers individually, and teams form on TAIKAI (Matchmaking tab).
- Each person can be on **only one team**, and each team submits **only one solution**.
- Each team names a **team coordinator**, who is the contact with EUSPA.
- Age **18+** at the start (27 Nov 2026).
- Nationality: an **EU member state** or a **Horizon Europe associated country** (or one negotiating association). Companies must be established there.
- Excluded:
  - Staff and contractors of EUSPA, the European Commission or ESA working on EGNSS or Copernicus, and their close family
  - Anyone already getting EU funding for the same project
  - People under EU sanctions
  - People in an exclusion situation under the EU Financial Regulation (Art. 136/141)
- Taking part is free. Your own costs aren't reimbursed.

## Supported EU space programmes (from cassini.eu)
- **Copernicus**: Earth observation (Sentinels, services)
- **Galileo**: EU satellite navigation and timing (includes OSNMA signal authentication and the EWSS emergency warning service)
- **EGNOS**: augmentation system that improves GNSS accuracy and reliability
- **IRIS²**: future secure EU satellite connectivity constellation
- **GOVSATCOM**: secure government satellite communications
- **EU SST**: Space Surveillance and Tracking (space situational awareness)
