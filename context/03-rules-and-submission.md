# Rules and submission requirements

Source: *Rules of Contest – Cassini Hackathons Prize, after Corrigendum 1* (EUSPA, 2024). This is still the version cassini.eu links for the 2026 edition. A copy is in `sources/rules-of-contest.pdf`, with extracted text in `sources/rules-of-contest.txt`. The schedule in the PDF only covers editions 7–10; the 2026 dates are in `01-hackathon.md`.

## Purpose of the contest (what EUSPA wants)
- Solutions must **use services and data from EU space programmes** (Galileo, Copernicus, GOVSATCOM and so on) and **promote synergies between them**.
- Combining them with non-space deep tech is allowed and encouraged: AI/ML, data analytics, GIS, IoT and so on.
- Proposals should deliver **new, innovative applications with commercial and social benefit, impact and a clear market uptake**.

## Submission (Stage 1)
- **Deadline: Sun 29 Nov 2026, 15:00 CET**, which is 15:00 on the last day of the weekend.
- **Submit only through TAIKAI**, in English, and **mark the project as "completed"**. Anything not marked completed by the deadline is ineligible. Submit early in case of IT problems.

The project page must contain:
1. **Short project summary (max 2 pages)**
2. **Team coordinator**, the contact person for EUSPA
3. **All team members**, with a short bio and their role
4. **Link to a video or screen recording** of the prototype, if you have one (strongly recommended)
5. **How it links to the theme and to at least one challenge**
6. **How it uses space data, information, services and signals**
7. **APIs, SDKs, frameworks and libraries used**
8. **Any unique hardware used**
9. Optional: **link to a GitHub repo** with open-source code

## Stage 2: Demo Day (2 Dec 2026)
- Online live pitch in English, judged on the same criteria.
- The jury produces a provisional ranking. **The top 5 must send these within 5 working days** to prizes@euspa.europa.eu, with the subject *"EUSPA-Cassini Hackathons-Submission of documents"*:
  - **Declaration of Honour** (Annex I.B template), one per team member; a company member sends one for the company
  - **Legal Entity Form** with an ID copy for each member. Black out the photo and all personal data except name, date of birth and nationality. A company sends a registry extract.
  - **Financial Identification Form**, one per team, for the coordinator. **The prize is paid to that person.**
- If a top-3 team fails the checks, the next team moves up.
- The award is decided by EUSPA's Authorising Officer and notified within about 15 days. Payment is in EUR within 30 days.

## Intellectual property and obligations
- **The team keeps full IP and exclusive commercial rights.**
- Winners must show **Galileo, Copernicus or EUSPA branding** in marketing for the product, for example a logo on the product page.
- Winners give EUSPA and the EU a non-exclusive licence to use their name and logo to promote the prize. EUSPA may publish the winner's name, location, prize amount, team name and project description. You can ask for this to be waived for security or commercial reasons.
- Winners may call themselves winners, but **may not imply they are endorsed** by EUSPA or the EU.
- The prize can't be assigned to anyone else without EUSPA's written consent.

## Other conditions worth knowing
- **Security (§8.8):** projects involving security-sensitive information that would need EU classification **cannot be awarded a prize**. Keep demos on open data and avoid sensitive operational details, which matters for defence and border ideas.
- EUSPA can withdraw the prize and recover payments for false information, fraud, ineligibility or serious breach of the rules.
- EUSPA, OLAF and the Court of Auditors may audit winners.
- Registering means accepting the rules.
- Rules questions go to prizes@euspa.europa.eu with subject "Cassini Hackathons Prize Contest", at least 6 working days before the deadline (already passed for this edition).

## Submission checklist (practical)
- [ ] TAIKAI project created, team members added, coordinator chosen
- [ ] Challenge(s) selected under "Categories"
- [ ] 2-page summary: problem, users, solution, space data, business model, team
- [ ] Section on how the project links to the theme and challenge
- [ ] Section on space data: which Copernicus and Galileo services, and **what each one is used for**
- [ ] List of APIs, SDKs and libraries
- [ ] Hardware (or "none")
- [ ] Demo video (2–3 min screen recording) uploaded and linked
- [ ] GitHub repo public (optional)
- [ ] **Marked as COMPLETED before 15:00 CET Sunday**
- [ ] Pitch deck ready for the local pitches straight after the deadline
