# How projects are judged

The same criteria apply at the local stage and at Demo Day. Source: Rules of Contest §4.5 and the TAIKAI overview.

| Criterion | Points | Weight | What judges ask (TAIKAI wording) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Relevance** of the prototype and business idea to the theme and challenge | 0–5 | 33% | Does it address one of the three challenges? Does it use **Copernicus, Galileo and/or EGNOS** data and signals? |
| **Innovativeness** of the prototype and value for future users and customers | 0–5 | 33% | How innovative is it compared with what's already on the market? **Is there a viable business case?** |
| **Quality of the team** | 0–5 | 33% | Do the team's technical, business and creative skills match what the project needs? |

- Maximum 15 points. The highest score wins.
- **Tie-break:** a higher Relevance score wins. If that's also tied, a higher Innovativeness score wins. **So Relevance matters most.**

## What this means in practice
**Relevance (maximise first)**
- Name the challenge and repeat its own wording, for example "map conflict damage", "routes for goods and people using Galileo" or "detect smuggling routes".
- Space data must be **essential**, not decoration: "without Sentinel-1 this doesn't work".
- Use both Copernicus and Galileo where it's natural. EUSPA explicitly values synergies between EU space components.
- Keep the peace, resilience and security angle visible.

**Innovativeness and business**
- Show you know the competition, and why your approach is different (see `06-competition.md`).
- Name a **paying customer** (who buys it, and why now) and a simple revenue model.
- "Value for future users": one concrete user story beats a generic platform.
- Judges see many generic "AI crisis dashboards" and "GNSS spoofing detectors" (see the competition file), so specific beats broad.

**Team**
- Show roles clearly on the project page: tech, data/EO, business, design/pitch.
- Teams that are only developers lose points. Someone owning the business case and pitch is worth a lot.
- Mention relevant experience (domain, EO, web, previous hackathons).

**Prototype and demo**
- A working demo on real Copernicus/Galileo data, even for a narrow case, beats slides.
- Replaying a real past event (a flood, a fire, a strike) is honest and looks live.
- The video is in the submission list. Record it before Sunday 13:00.
